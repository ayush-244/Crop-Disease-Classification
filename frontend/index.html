About This Project

CropCare AI is an advanced machine learning system designed to help farmers and agricultural professionals quickly identify crop diseases from leaf images. Using state-of-the-art deep learning technology, our system can detect and classify various plant diseases with high accuracy.

The system is trained on the PlantVillage dataset, containing thousands of images of healthy and diseased crop leaves. It supports multiple crops including tomatoes, potatoes, and peppers, detecting diseases like bacterial spot, early blight, late blight, and more.
